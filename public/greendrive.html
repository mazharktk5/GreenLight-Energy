SkyDrive

SkyDrive

Vehicle-to-Home (V2H) &
Vehicle-to-Grid (V2G)

 

Transforms electric vehicles into mobile energy storage assets for homes and the grid. Most traditional solar systems shut down during a power outage if they don’t have a dedicated battery. With our new V2H & V2G technology, your EV becomes your home’s private grid.

  • Massive Capacity: While home batteries are often 5–10kWh, an EV offers 40kWh to 60kWh, enough to power your home for days.
  • Retrofit Ready: Perfect for homeowners with existing solar who want energy independence without buying a separate stationary battery.
  • Seamless Integration: Power your lights, appliances, and security systems directly from your car.

Key Capabilities

  • Backup power from EV during outages
  • Peak load support & energy shifting
  • Smart, wall-mounted architecture

Applications

  • EV-enabled homes
  • Smart buildings

Target Segments

  • Residential EV owners
  • Smart city infrastructure
